Proper Greek Gyros in Middlesbrough, Now Delivered to Your Door

The Greek Spot 4 min read Middlesbrough

There is a new reason to look forward to dinner in the town centre. Greek gyros in Middlesbrough now arrives hot at your door, chargrilled to order and wrapped the moment you tap send. The Greek Spot on Dundas Street has opened an evening service built around one thing done properly, the gyros.

By day the place is a Greek bakery and cafe, all hand stretched phyllo and shaken coffee. Come 5pm the grill takes over. From Monday to Saturday, 5pm to 9pm, we cook gyros, chicken gyros and skepasti to order and send them out for delivery.

Here is exactly what each one is, how it is made, and how to have it on your table tonight.

What a Greek gyros actually is

A gyros is meat cooked slowly on a vertical rotisserie, shaved off in thin ribbons as it crisps, then piled into a warm, soft pita. Ours goes in with tzatziki, tomato, onion and chips, all wrapped together so every bite has a little of everything.

The chicken gyros is the same idea made with chargrilled chicken, a touch lighter but just as loaded. Both are cooked to order, which is the whole point. Nothing sits waiting under a lamp. When your order comes through, that is when it hits the grill.

  • Gyros: meat shaved from the rotisserie with tzatziki, tomato, onion and chips in a warm pita
  • Chicken gyros: the same generous wrap, made with chargrilled chicken

Skepasti, the one worth knowing about

Skepasti means covered, and it is the move for anyone who likes things crisp. We take the same gyros filling, fold the pita over the top of it and press the whole parcel flat on the grill until the outside turns golden and crunchy.

You end up with a sealed, toasted pocket rather than an open wrap, warm through the middle and crisp at the edges. If you have only ever had a standard gyros, this is the one to try next.

Daytime bakery, evening grill

The Greek Spot is really two places in one. From 9am to 4pm, Monday to Saturday, it is a Greek bakery and cafe in the middle of Middlesbrough, a short walk from Centre Square, the Cleveland Centre and Teesside University. That is when the counter fills with bougatsa, baklava, kataifi nests and freshly shaken freddo coffee.

When the cafe closes, the evening service begins. Same kitchen, same care, a different menu built around the grill. So the daytime is pastries and coffee to sit in or take away, and the evening is Greek food delivery Middlesbrough can order from the sofa. Whichever half of the day you catch us, it is made fresh that day.

Good to know

When can I order gyros for delivery?

Every evening from Monday to Saturday, 5pm to 9pm. The daytime cafe runs separately from 9am to 4pm, but the gyros, chicken gyros and skepasti are an evening only service.

How do I order Greek food delivery from The Greek Spot?

Through Uber Eats. The evening menu is delivery only and exclusively on Uber Eats, so open the app or use the link on this page, add your order and we cook it to order.

What is the difference between a gyros and skepasti?

A gyros is served open in a warm pita with tzatziki, tomato, onion and chips. Skepasti takes that same filling, folds the pita over it and presses it flat on the grill until it is crisp. Same flavours, crunchier finish.

Can I get gyros at the cafe during the day?

No. During the day, 9am to 4pm, we are a Greek bakery and cafe serving pastries, cakes and coffee. The grill and the gyros are an evening delivery service from 5pm to 9pm.
